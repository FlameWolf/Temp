About the model

The magnet is a uniformly magnetised bar seen in cross-section. The Earth’s field is its horizontal part. The compass needle and every grain of powder line up with the resultant of the two, so near the magnet its field takes over, and far away the Earth’s does.

Field lines are spaced by equal amounts of flux, so where they crowd together the field is stronger. A null point is where the two fields are equal and opposite and cancel.
