Light through cross-reeded glass

A distant lamp seen through two crossed sets of reeds. Raise the interaction between the reeds to watch the square split into a star inside a rounded outline.

0.15

Model surface: h = cos X + cos Y + c cos X cos Y, where X and Y advance by 2π from one reed to the next and c is the reed interaction. Each point on the glass deflects the lamp's light in proportion to its slope (the small-angle approximation), so the pattern is a histogram of the surface slopes. The bright lines are caustics, where many points share almost the same slope.
